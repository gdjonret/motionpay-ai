# Exercise visual credits

## Workout Guide 1.0.0

Glute Bridge, Incline Push-up, One-Arm Dumbbell Row, and Dead Bug frames are bundled from `@bryllim/workout-guide@1.0.0`. Frames for the remaining library exercises (294 entries in `src/data/catalog.json` with an `illustration` slug) are loaded unmodified at runtime from the package's versioned jsDelivr CDN (`https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@bryllim/workout-guide@1.0.0/`). Their movement instructions and difficulty levels were written for MotionPay and are draft content pending review.

Artwork by Bryl Lim, https://bryllim.com. Where identified in the package manifest, first-pose artwork is based on Everkinetic, https://github.com/everkinetic/data. Assets are CC BY-SA 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ . Package software is MIT. Full notices and asset license are included in the installed package's ATTRIBUTION.md, LICENSE-ASSETS, and manifest.json.

Frames are unchanged. MotionPay sequences them as a three-frame demonstration; these are illustrations, not smooth GIFs or muscle-highlighted animations. Glute Bridge's original pose is https://github.com/everkinetic/data/blob/main/dist/svg/0109-tension.svg . Any adaptations of the artwork must retain the asset license and appropriate attribution.
